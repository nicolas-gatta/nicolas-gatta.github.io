# Organization logos

Local copies identify the organizations listed in the portfolio's experience timeline.

- `google.svg`: [Google's official branding asset](https://www.gstatic.com/images/branding/googlelogo/svg/googlelogo_clr_74x24px.svg).
- `ibm.svg`: positive 8-bar geometry extracted from [IBM Design Language's construction diagram](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/a41bb6fc86bf12f915d28f4c34f33d74/8bar.svg). The diagram's grid and annotations are omitted; the mark's geometry is preserved. See [IBM Design Language](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/ibm-logos/8-bar/).
- `takeda.png`: [Takeda's official media asset](https://assets-dam.takeda.com/image/upload/v1678214063/Media%20Resources/Takeda_Logo.png), linked from its [media resources](https://www.takeda.com/newsroom/media-resources/).

The marks belong to their respective owners. The airship links to the CV-sourced experience section.
